About
Thomas Igou.

Thomas Igou is a French writer living in Stockholm. He began writing while living in New York. His personal life inspires his work, especially short stories, his favourite form, while he continues to explore other styles. His influences include Kurt Vonnegut, Stephen King, J.R.R. Tolkien and Jay McInerney. He is a father of five.

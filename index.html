A note on this archive

Writing across
forms and years.

This archive gathers Thomas Igou’s writing across short fiction, poetry, theatre, screen and longer work. It is both a reading room and a record of the work in progress.

The catalogue is being assembled from drafts and collections. Titles and status reflect the current inventory and may evolve as the archive grows.
